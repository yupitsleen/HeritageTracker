## Bonus

**Verified clean, notably:** Gaza War Cemetery entry checks out end-to-end (3,691 burials ≈ "more than 3,600"; Guardian story 4 Feb 2026; WWII Australian/British/Polish sections leveled) — Wikipedia now has a sourced section on it. Souq al-Zawiya's specific numbers (250 m, 65 buildings, 1,150+ shops) match the ICOMOS/Jordan Times heritage alert verbatim. Rantisi (opened 2008, 124 beds, only pediatric oncology/dialysis), Indonesian Hospital (2016, reopened 1 June 2024), Abu Hussein school (28 killed/160 wounded, 17 Oct 2024), AISG ($5M campus), ESNCM 2025 IMC award, Shahwan (10,000+ artifacts, 30 yrs), Al-Azhar (18 Oct 1991, 12 colleges/80 disciplines) all confirmed.

**Internal duplicates to reconcile:**
- `ataa-library-beit-hanoun` (2023) and `ibby-library-beit-hanoun` (2014) are the same IBBY-supported institution — fine as two destruction events, but they should cross-reference each other.
- Three IUG entries (`islamic-university-central-library` 2023, `islamic-university-gaza` 2023, `islamic-university-gaza-library` 2008); the two 2023 entries give different dates (Oct 9 library per LAP vs Oct 11 campus) — both defensible but worth a cross-note.
- `nasser-medical-complex` and `european-gaza-hospital` both claim "largest hospital in southern Gaza" — resolve in Nasser's favor.

**Notable missing sites encountered:** Grand Mosque of Khan Yunis (the actual 96-year-old mosque in the Aug 2024 Al Jazeera demolition footage — currently misattributed to Bani Saleh); Rafah Museum (Suhaila Shaheen, bombed 28 Oct 2023, salvage tent later bombed too — compelling documented story); Gaza City Central Archives (mentioned only inside the souq entry; centennial municipal documents, destroyed Nov 2023); Al-Fakhoora school (multiple mass-casualty strikes); Al-Nasr Children's Hospital; Al-Jawni school attack (Sept 2024).

**Better/easier authoritative sources:**
- Institute for Palestine Studies damage databases — per-site pages with dates/sources: gazahcsector.palestine-studies.org (health), gazaeducationsector.palestine-studies.org (education), gazacultrualsector.palestine-studies.org (culture).
- gigaza.org (GIG) — per-mosque/market damage pages with dates (already cited by some entries).
- Librarians and Archivists with Palestine report (librarianswithpalestine.org/gaza-report-2024) — canonical for the library entries; note it gives "first reported" dates, so several Dec 6 2023 "destroyed on" dates should really be "first reported on."
- UNESCO's verified damage-assessment list for the "UNESCO verified" claims; CWGC casualty database for war-cemetery figures; Scholars at Risk incident pages for universities.
