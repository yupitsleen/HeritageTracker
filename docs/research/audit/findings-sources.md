## Bonus

**Canonical sources that could replace scattered links:**
- **UNESCO assessment page** (https://www.unesco.org/en/gaza/assessment) — names all 164 verified sites by governorate, updated continually. Could be the single `official` source for every UNESCO-verified entry; the dataset's invented titles ("Gaza Heritage Damage Assessment", "Preliminary Damage Assessment of Cultural Sites in Gaza", "Gaza Strip Damage Assessment") should be normalized to the page's actual title, "Impact on cultural heritage".
- **Forensic Architecture — A Cartography of Genocide** (https://forensic-architecture.org/investigation/a-cartography-of-genocide) — the correct FA citation for war-period destruction; **Living Archaeology in Gaza** for pre-war archaeological documentation.
- **Librarians and Archivists with Palestine report** (already used, real URL) — canonical for the library/archive entries.
- **Heritage for Peace situation reports** at heritageforpeace.org — the 23 sites citing the phantom "Cultural Heritage in Gaza: Destroyed and Damaged Sites Report" should be re-pointed at the actual 7 Nov 2023 preliminary report, or at its real press coverage already in the dataset (The Art Newspaper 2023-11-28; NPR 2023-12-03 https://www.npr.org/2023/12/03/1216200754/gaza-heritage-sites-destroyed-israel).
