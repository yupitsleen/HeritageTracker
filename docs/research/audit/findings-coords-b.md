- al-sardi-school-nuseirat — Airwars page 403-blocked; school not in OSM; try UNRWA installations CSV (see Bonus).
- rafah-refugee-camp-school-cluster — composite entry; no single building to verify.
- deir-el-balah-bronze-age-cemetery — Dothan excavation locus not published at building level; claimed point unverifiable.
- khalid-ibn-walid-mosque-al-qarara — no coordinate source found.
- great-mosque-khan-yunis — conflicting evidence: OSM "المسجد الكبير" at 31.3449, 34.2988 (390 m W of claimed) vs. reports/photos placing the 1928 Grand Mosque beside Barquq Castle (34.3033); needs imagery check.
- sheikh-eid-mosque-mughrabi-quarter — claimed = Mughrabi Quarter centroid (WP); quarter footprint ~150 m; building-level position of the (still-standing) mosque not found in a citable source. IDENTICAL flag stands.
- bou-medyan-zaouia — same centroid as above; zawiya's exact plot within the demolished quarter not published. Both points are inside the quarter (error likely <150 m), but the two sites should not share one coordinate.

Bulk coordinate sources for future geocoding:
- Wikidata SPARQL (P625) by sitelink — one query returned 16 of these sites (query.wikidata.org).
- OSM Overpass API — Gaza mosques/hospitals/museums/ruins well tagged; one bbox query returned ~200 POIs (overpass-api.de).
- Institute for Palestine Studies databases: gazacultrualsector.palestine-studies.org (cultural sector) and gazahcsector.palestine-studies.org (health sector) — per-site damage pages with locations.
- UNRWA installations dataset on HDX (data.humdata.org) — CSV with lat/lon for every UNRWA school/shelter; solves al-sardi and the Rafah school cluster.
- Forensic Architecture "Living Archaeology in Gaza" platform — georeferenced Gaza heritage sites.
- Palestine Remembered + Zochrot — per-village GPS for all 1948 depopulated villages (covers Lifta, Iqrit, Bir'im, Zir'in, Qaqun, Imwas, Yalo, Bayt Nuba).
- UNESCO Gaza damage assessment (unesco.org/en/gaza/assessment) — authoritative damaged-site list, though without published coordinates.
